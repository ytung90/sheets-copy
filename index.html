Sheets Copy

Settings

Connection

The Web app URL from your Google Apps Script deployment (ends in /exec).

Written into the name column when you press Done.

Sheet profile

Open the sheet on the right tab, then Share → Copy link, or copy it from the address bar.

Columns

In the order you paste them. Ranges work too: C-E, H

On Done, the date and time go in the timestamp column and your name in the name column (leave blank to skip).

Which rows to show

Upper/lower case and spaces are ignored. Leave the column blank to show every row that has no timestamp yet.
